Common questions

How do I throw? Drag to aim, then flick upward. A clean, straight flick at the right speed hits where you aim. Replay the tutorial in Settings > How to play.

How do I restore purchases? Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel Dartix Pro? In iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

How do I delete my online data? In the app: Settings > Delete my online data.
