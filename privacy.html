Privacy Policy

Dartix: Darts Game & 501 · Effective date: November 2, 2026

Dartix ("the app") is made by Shaker Badal Shukur Shukur ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information. In short: we collect only what online play needs, never sell or share it for advertising, and you can delete it in the app at any time.

Data stored on your device

Your progress, cash, darts, stats, achievements, season pass progress and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Online play

When you use Play Online, the app creates an anonymous account for you with our database provider (Supabase). No email, phone number or real name is needed. We store: a random account ID, the player name you chose, your dart set and cosmetics, and the darts thrown in your online matches (used to score the match and for the weekly leaderboard). Player names are filtered for offensive words.

Reports and blocks

If you report or block another player, we store the report (who reported whom, the match and the name shown) to keep online play safe. Repeatedly reported names are replaced with "Player".

Deleting your online data

Go to Settings > Delete my online data. This permanently deletes your online matches, leaderboard entries, reports, blocks and your anonymous account. Your offline progress on the device is not affected.

Purchases

Payments for Dartix Pro, the Gold Pass and cash packs are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. To confirm and restore purchases we use a purchase-management provider (RevenueCat), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, not your name or contact details.

No ads, no tracking

Dartix shows no advertising and uses no analytics or tracking tools. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Dartix is suitable for everyone. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; online play needs no personal details.

Your rights

You can delete your online data in the app at any time, or contact us to ask what is stored about your account ID.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
